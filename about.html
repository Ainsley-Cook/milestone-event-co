About Milestone

We started Milestone because good events shouldn't feel like work.

Founded in 2016 out of a spare bedroom in Salt Lake City, Milestone Event Co. has grown into a nine-person team trusted by brands, instructors, and communities across 40+ neighborhoods — without ever losing the hands-on energy we started with.

Our Story

From a neighborhood craft swap to 250+ events.

Milestone's founder, Rachel Ordoñez, started small: a candle-making night in her apartment for a dozen neighbors who couldn't find anything fun to do on a Tuesday. It sold out in two days. Then it happened again. Within a year, Rachel was booking instructors, hunting down venues, and turning her side project into a real event company.

Today, Milestone plans pop-up fitness classes, craft nights, and brand activations for local businesses, national brands, and instructors building their own following — plus private events for companies who want their team party to feel like an actual party.

We're based in Salt Lake City and travel constantly — roughly half the events we produce happen outside Utah.

Our Process

How we take an event from idea to sold out.

01

Discovery & concept

We start with your vibe, audience, and budget, then build a proposal with real venue and instructor options — not vague ideas.

02

Planning & booking

Your dedicated planner books instructors, vendors, and permits, with weekly updates so you're never guessing what's next.

03

Promotion support

Need help filling the room? We help design your RSVP page, social content, and day-of run-of-show together.

04

Event day execution

Our team runs the floor in person, handling every cue and hiccup so you can actually enjoy your own event.

05

Wrap report

Within a week you'll get a recap with attendance, spend, and vendor notes — plus what we'd do again next time.

06

Total transparency

No hidden markups on vendor contracts. You see exactly what instructors, venues, and supplies cost, plus our flat planning fee.
