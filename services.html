Common Questions

Pricing & process FAQ

Is the package price the total cost of my event?+

No — package pricing covers our planning fee. Venue rental, instructors, and materials are billed separately at the vendor's rate, with no markup from us. We'll build a full budget estimate during your consultation.

Can you find an instructor or teacher for our pop-up?+

Yes — booking the right yoga instructor, fitness coach, or craft teacher is one of the things we do most. We work from a vetted local roster and can also bring in a specific instructor you already have in mind.

Do you handle permits for outdoor or public pop-ups?+

Yes — permits, insurance, and venue approvals are handled as part of our Pop-Up Permits & Insurance add-on, so you don't have to navigate city paperwork yourself.

Can you plan something private, just for our company?+

Absolutely. Private Company Events are one of our four core categories — team celebrations, holiday parties, and offsites planned with the same energy as our public pop-ups, just closed to your guest list.

How far in advance should we book?+

We recommend 6-8 weeks for Essentials and Signature-level events, and 3-4 months for Executive-level, multi-day productions. That said, we've pulled off pop-ups with as little as two weeks' notice — ask us about rush availability.

Not sure which package fits?

Send us your event details and we'll recommend a package and rough budget within one business day.
